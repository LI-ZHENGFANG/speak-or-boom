Speak or Boom — voiceover provenance

Developer and copyright owner of the application: LI ZHENGFANG.
The 1,328 clips in the five everyday scenarios were generated locally with Kokoro-82M v1.0, American English,
using af_heart, af_bella and am_michael styles, native generation speed 0.95.
These are synthetic voices, not human actor or original film recordings.

Model project and licensing information (Apache-2.0):
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
ONNX conversion used for asset generation:
https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX
Generation wrapper:
https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx

The model, generator and its dependencies are not included in or required by
the browser application. Readers only play the bundled MP3 files.
